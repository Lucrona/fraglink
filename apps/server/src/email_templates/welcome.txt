1. Get the desktop app
The small Windows tray app counts your hours in every game it detects, whatever launcher you use. Hours only count while it's signed in and connected.

2. Bring your Steam hours
Link Steam in Settings > Connections and your playtime is imported, then refreshed daily. Your Steam game details need to be public.
Link Steam: https://playfraglink.com/settings#connections

3. Playing WoW? Add your /played
Link Battle.net, install the Fraglink /played addon from CurseForge, type /played in game, then upload it on the WoW page.
Get the addon: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/fraglink-played
Upload /played: https://playfraglink.com/connect/wow

4. Add your friends
See who's online and what they're playing, with a pop-up when they come online or start a game.
Find friends: https://playfraglink.com/friends

5. Make your profile yours
Add a picture, an about me and your gaming rig.
Edit profile: https://playfraglink.com/settings#account
