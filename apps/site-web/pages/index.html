Friends, live

See who's online and what they're playing right now, with a quiet pop-up when a friend starts a game. Invisible means invisible.

Hours that add up

Play time is counted while the app is connected, game by game. Your profile shows the past 7 days and your all-time totals.

Light and private

Lives in the tray and uses almost nothing. It never reads game memory or your keystrokes. Export or delete your data any time.
