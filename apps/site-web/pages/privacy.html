Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Fraglink is a small project. This page says plainly what we collect and why. We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking scripts.

What the app collects

The Windows app watches the list of running programs and compares it with Fraglink's own games list. It never reads game memory, never injects anything into games, and never records keystrokes, screenshots or chat. Signed out, it makes no network connections.

When you're signed in and connected, the server receives and stores:

  • Game ids for the game you're playing right now (for example wow or a Steam app id). For a program you tagged as a game yourself, its .exe name.
  • Play time: start and end of each session the server sees while you're connected. Nothing is recorded offline.
  • Presence: online, away or invisible, your current game and since when. We keep only your latest presence, not a history of it. Invisible shows you as offline to everyone.
  • WoW realm: if you play World of Warcraft, the realm you're on, shown to friends next to the game. You can hide it in the app.
  • Game suggestions: if you choose to suggest a program as a game, we receive its .exe name and the name you suggested so we can review it. Until a game is approved it shows to others as "Playing a game".

Your account

  • Username and display name (visible to other signed-in users), an optional email address, and your password stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Sign-in tokens (stored hashed) and the names of devices you signed in on.
  • Your friends, friend requests and block list.
  • Hours you import yourself, and Steam playtime if you link Steam.

Who can see what

  • Friends see your status and current game, and, if you share play history (on by default, change it in Account settings), your games, hours and recent activity.
  • Other signed-in users see only your username, display name, join date and friend count.
  • Profiles aren't public on the web.

Where it's stored and who else gets it

Data is stored on a server in Australia (BinaryLane). Email to support@playfraglink.com is routed by Cloudflare. If you link Steam, we ask Steam's API for your playtime. Game icons may load from Steam's image servers, which can see your IP address. Nobody else receives your data, except where the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

The website keeps you signed in with a token stored in your browser. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies.

Your choices

  • Export everything from Account settings at any time.
  • Delete your account there too. Deletion is immediate and removes your sessions, hours, friends and presence.
  • Sign out in the app to stop sending anything.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@playfraglink.com. If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll say so in the app and here before it takes effect.
