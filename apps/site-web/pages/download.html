Getting started

  1. Run the installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for a new app: choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Fraglink starts in the tray (next to the clock). Click it and choose Sign in or Create account.
  3. Add your mates by their Fraglink username. Then just play, your hours show up on your profile.

It updates itself. It's still in beta so there'll be some rough edges, let us know on Discord.
