Getting started

  1. Run the installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice for a new app: choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Fraglink starts in the tray (next to the clock). Click it and choose Sign in or Create account.
  3. Add friends by their Fraglink username. Play something: your hours show up on your profile.

The app updates itself. It's in early testing, so expect rough edges, and tell us about them on Discord.
