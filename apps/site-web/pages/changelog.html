WebsiteImported hours keep counting 27 October 2026

  • Hours you play after a WoW /played or Steam import now add on top of it, so your total goes up straight away. Importing again replaces the starting point, so nothing counts twice.
  • Steam hours are no longer refreshed automatically. Press Import hours in Settings › Connections whenever you want to update them.
  • WoW /played uploads now remind you to log in to every character first, and a lower upload never lowers your hours: we keep the higher total. Deleted characters keep their hours.
  • The upload page no longer offers to recount your WoW hours. If your total looks wrong, contact support and staff can recount it from your characters' /played.
  • Time Fraglink tracks in WoW menus and loading screens is now kept: when a new /played upload grew less than the time the app tracked, the difference stays on top of your total.
  • Help pages, the FAQ and the welcome email now explain how imported and tracked hours combine.
  • Hidden WoW characters no longer show on your own profile either, so it looks exactly like what visitors see. Unhide them under Manage WoW.
  • Feature your top 3 WoW characters: pick them and their order under Manage WoW (searchable, separate for Retail and Forever). They show first on your profile; with none picked your most played show first.
  • WoW characters are now matched by their Battle.net character id: a renamed or realm-transferred character counts once, a character you sold or moved to another Battle.net account counts for its new owner only, and deleted characters keep their hours. Your characters are rechecked on every upload.
  • WoW Forever isn't released yet, so Forever /played uploads are closed for now; your Forever hours are tracked by the app until it launches.
