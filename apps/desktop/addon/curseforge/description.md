Logs your /played time for each character so you can add it to your profile on [playfraglink.com](https://playfraglink.com). Works in both Retail and WoW Forever.

Nothing is sent while you play. The addon saves to a file on your PC, and you upload it on the site whenever you like.

## Install

Unzip into your `_retail_/Interface/AddOns` or `_forever_/Interface/AddOns` folder (or both), then restart WoW or type `/reload`.

## How to use it

1. Log in to each character you want to track.
2. Type `/reload` or log out so WoW saves the file.
3. On playfraglink.com, go to your WoW page, pick Retail or Forever, and upload `WTF/Account/<ACCOUNT>/SavedVariables/FraglinkPlayed.lua`.

Link your Battle.net account to get your Retail characters verified.

Type `/fragq` to hide the chat message.
