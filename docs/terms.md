## Playing fair

Fraglink only works if the hours are real and people feel safe. So please don't:
- **Cheat the hours:** fake play time (for example by running a program that pretends to be a game), or add imported hours that aren't true.
- **Spoof or tamper:** change, imitate or interfere with the Fraglink app, its connection to our server, or the data it sends, including imported game counters such as World of Warcraft `/played` data.
- **Scrape or overload:** collect data from Fraglink with bots or scripts, get around rate limits, or try to break, overload or get unauthorised access to the service.
- **Abuse people:** harass, bully, threaten or spam anyone, including with friend requests.
- **Impersonate:** pretend to be someone else, another player, or Fraglink staff.
- **Post harmful content** in your username, nickname, profile picture, bio or any other part of your profile. That includes anything illegal, hateful, sexual, violent or threatening, personal information about other people, and anything that sexualises children (which we report to the authorities).
- If we learn of a threat to someone's life or safety, we'll tell the police.

Hours count while a game is running, just like other trackers, so you don't need to worry about stepping away for a bit.

To keep Fraglink fair and safe, we may:
- **Review and reset hours** that look suspicious or impossible, including removing individual sessions or imported hours.
- **Remove or change content** that breaks these terms, like a profile picture, bio or nickname, and rename a username that breaks them.
- **Suspend or ban accounts** that break these terms, or that we need to act on to protect other players or comply with the law.

A person reviews every report. The only automatic check is a block list that stops a removed profile picture being uploaded again.

We'll act in proportion to what happened. We'll always tell you what we did, why, and how to ask us to look again, unless that would put someone at risk or the law doesn't allow it. If you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll take another look.
